
HEART DISEASE RISK ANALYSIS SUMMARY
-----------------------------------

1. Demographics:
   - Older individuals and males show higher prevalence of heart disease.
   - Age and sex are top-ranked predictors in both Random Forest and Logistic Regression.

2. Lifestyle:
   - Smoking and low exercise levels are associated with increased heart disease risk.
   - Their model importance is moderate, suggesting secondary contribution.

3. Medical History:
   - Hypertension, diabetes, and cholesterol levels show strong separation between disease and non-disease groups.
   - These align with clinical expectations and have high feature importance.

4. Clinical Measurements:
   - Resting BP and Max HR are among the most predictive features.
   - Patients with lower Max HR or higher BP tend to have heart disease.
